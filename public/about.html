ABOUT KODERMA WORK FINDER

Koderma Jile Ka Apna Digital Rozgar Platform

Hamara uddeshya Koderma jile ke har gaon aur prakhand ke karigaron, kisanon aur daily wage workers ko seedhe kaam dene walon se jodna hai—bina kisi commission ya dalali ke.

👷 Workers (काम करने वाले) Ke Liye

  • Bilkul free mein apna Worker Profile banayein.
  • 41+ kaam ki shreniyon (Rajmistri, Tractor Driver, Dhan Ropai, AC Repairing, Shop Work) mein apna hunar dikhayein.
  • Apni marzi se contact permission aur work availability set karein.
  • Apne gaon aur block ke naye jobs dekhein aur turant apply karein.

🏢 Employers (काम देने वाले) Ke Liye

  • Ghar, kheti, dukan ya construction site ke liye turant naya kaam post karein.
  • GPS "Near Me" aur block filter se apne paas ke bharosemand workers khojein.
  • Seedhe Phone Call, WhatsApp ya Website ke zariye sampark karein.
  • 0% Commission — poora paisa seedhe kaam karne wale ko milta hai.
